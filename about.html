About

I am a 4th-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of New Hampshire. My research focuses on harnessing Large Language Models (LLMs) to accelerate scientific discovery and improve AI systems across materials science, healthcare, and education. I practice human-focused design and optimization, working with experts to turn real needs into effective intelligent systems.

Research Interests

  • Core Areas: NLP, Large Language Models, Computational Linguistics
  • Methods: Information Extraction, Explainability, RLHF/Preference Tuning, Bayesian Optimization
  • Applications: Materials Science, Healthcare, Social Science, Education
